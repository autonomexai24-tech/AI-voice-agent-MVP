---

# Product Direction

Build a generic realtime AI receptionist platform for businesses.

Target businesses:
- clinics
- salons
- gyms
- restaurants
- real estate offices
- legal offices
- local service businesses

The platform should allow businesses to:
- deploy AI receptionists
- configure receptionist behavior
- manage multilingual conversations
- handle bookings
- escalate to humans
- customize conversational style

without technical knowledge.

---

# Primary Product Goal

The platform should feel like:
- a real receptionist
- not an AI assistant

The core success metric is:

> “That sounded like a real receptionist.”

This matters more than:
- extreme intelligence
- long GPT responses
- agent autonomy
- complex AI behavior

The platform prioritizes:
- realism
- responsiveness
- operational reliability
